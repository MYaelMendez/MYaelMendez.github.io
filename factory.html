WHAT IS TRUE HERE

Every number on this page comes from ffprobe (dimensions, frames, bitrate) or SupervisorVideo (luminance, contrast, motion, verdict) — measured, not asserted.

Each video carries a SHA-256 receipt of its supervision metrics. Same video → same receipt.

The vision is deterministic computer vision (statistics + geometry), not machine learning. It is auditable: every verdict traces to a measurable threshold.
