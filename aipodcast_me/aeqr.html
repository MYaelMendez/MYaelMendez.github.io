Canonical RecordVersioned · Attributed · Explicit

A canonical æQR record carries a version, target, capability, intent, principal, authorization policy, and witness requirement. The QR image encodes a short HTTPS resolver URL — not raw credentials or raw capability bindings.

Example record

{
  "protocol": "aeqr",
  "version": "0.1",
  "target": "ae://city/business/loft/start",
  "capability": "startabusiness",
  "intent": "open",
  "principal": "human-required",
  "authorization": "explicit",
  "witness": "required"
}
The resolver validates the record, checks permissions when opened, and routes to the correct loft. Scanning the QR code alone never grants authority.
æQR makes the nested environment discoverable and portable.
It gives intelligent work an address — a way to recover its context, a path to reuse, and a route to publication. Every widget carries its own æQR. Every loft can become a tower. Every tower can become a city.
