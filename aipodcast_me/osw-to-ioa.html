From Promptification to AgentsThe gap

Promptification makes language an interface. Agentic engineering makes intent operational. Internet of Agents makes capabilities interoperable. The transformation isn't more elaborate prompting — it's a standardized passage from human intent to an authorized, addressable capability with verifiable results.

1

🔍 Discover

Find a published capability in the city. Each discoverable tower, loft, or widget carries its æQR address and a versioned manifest.

2

🔐 Authorize

Human principal grants bounded permission. Authority never propagates through context inheritance — it is always explicitly granted at the boundary.

3

🛸 Execute

$mcp compiles intent into a real capability contract — not another prompt. The contract dispatches to the authorized agent or model.

4

🧾 Witness

Every consequential action produces a traceable receipt: who authorized it, what ran, what changed, what it cost. SHA-256 verifiable.

5

📦 Publish ↺

Results are published to a durable address. Anyone can fork, adapt, and re-execute under their own authority. Context propagates; authority does not.
