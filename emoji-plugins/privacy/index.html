Privacy and data-handling disclosure

These four packages contain skill instructions and local assets rather than a custom server, account connector or MCP tool. A skill does not independently collect device clipboard contents or read accounts. In a host application, prompts and material you elect to share may be processed by that host and any separately authorized tools under their own policies. The hosting provider and its request logging, analytics and retention practices have not yet been confirmed for the eventual public URL. A Cloudflare Workers build check was reported for this pull request, but that check alone does not identify the production host. The draft pages contain no first-party sign-in or submission form. This description does not establish host-side retention, deletion or training controls.

  • Production hosting, infrastructure logs and retention have not been verified; review before publication
  • Do not share sensitive information in public issue reports
  • Publisher-specific legal privacy review remains pending
