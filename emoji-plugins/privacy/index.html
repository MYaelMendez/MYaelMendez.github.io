Privacy and data-handling disclosure

These four packages contain skill instructions and local assets rather than a custom server, account connector or MCP tool. A skill does not independently collect device clipboard contents or read accounts. In a host application, prompts and material you elect to share may be processed by that host and any separately authorized tools under their own policies. Pages served by GitHub Pages may be subject to GitHub's standard infrastructure logging; this site does not present its own sign-in or submission form. This description does not establish host-side retention, deletion or training controls.

  • We have not verified every host runtime or external service's data practices
  • Do not share sensitive information in public issue reports
  • Publisher-specific legal privacy review remains pending
