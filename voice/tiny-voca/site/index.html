Built for the Internet of Agents

Agents can speak, but they do not sign. Each voice is bound to a principal and a compute layer. The human remains root; the agent executes under approval; the robot is an executor with a body, never an authority source.

One package. Every surface.

tts_speak

Local English speech, 24 kHz WAV, canonical F2/M2 alias resolution, and a TTS://SPEAK receipt.

tts_stick_lesson

Three narrated Asimov lessons on the unchanged upstream stick-scene runtime.
