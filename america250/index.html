What could ownership support?

No measured cohort or revenue is loaded. Change the assumptions to explore annual business activity in USD. These are illustrative scenarios, not forecasts or GDP estimates.

Operating owners = target × activation × retention. Revenue = operating owners × sales. Surplus before tax = revenue × gross margin − operating owners × (other operating costs + compute). No extra multiplier; revenue and surplus are not additive.
