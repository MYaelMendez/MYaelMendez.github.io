◈ WHAT IT IS · WHAT IT IS NOTEVIDENCE ≠ AUTHORITY
It is
A registry of stable resource IDs → canonical routes + scope. A produce-and-verify gate with two real decoders. A deterministic audit → snapshot → compare → repair queue. An explicit, default-deny authority model.
It is not
A permission system. A renderer that trusts its own bytes. A claim that a fix landed. An implicit grant from scanning. A category placeholder pretending to be resolution.

◈ THE TWO LOAD-BEARING RULESCANON
1
A QR that has not been decoded back is not a QR — it is a picture of one. The gate decodes through two independent decoders. The rune magic æ: encodes fine and fails on scan; a single-decoder check passes exactly the artifact that fails in the field. Transport magic is ASCII (ae:); the æ identity lives in the payload.
2
Scanning never grants execution. resolve, read, and verify are open. publish, execute, and merge require an explicit, scoped capability. Planning a repair ("ready") is not proving it ("verified").
