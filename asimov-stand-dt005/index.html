ERRATUM

The earlier published trace claimed dt=0.005 was unstable at every gain and moved to dt=0.002. That was a coarse-grid artifact: Coarse grid: kd in {10,15,20,25,30}; kd=10 sagged at low kp, kd>=15 exploded, so the stable high-kp/low-kd region was never sampled.

False claim: At the shipped dt=0.005 the PD loop is unstable at every gain; dt must be lowered to 0.002.
Correction: A 63-point fine sweep at dt=0.005 found stable holds at kp=200..400 with kd=8..10. At kp=200/kd=10 the 1000-step hold has 2.3 mm root drift, 4.52 deg tilt, 0.058 rad/s max joint velocity.

not invalid — it is a valid PASS at dt=0.002, but its stated reason for changing dt was wrong.
