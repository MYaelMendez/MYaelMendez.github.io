He shows anything. He grants nothing. Wætz can plan a motion, render it, and explain it. He cannot sign it. Only a human can — that is not a limitation we are working around, it is the design.

WHAT THIS IS

A robot you can talk to, built from Menlo Research's open Asimov CAD and driven by Nous Research's Hermes Agent, governed by æ policy. Every answer he gives about motion carries a real policy decision: allow, require_approval, or deny.

The chat runs entirely in your browser. No account, no API key, no server call. The full 3D companion — 26 CAD links, a million triangles, rendered on an RTX — runs locally from three files and two pip installs.
