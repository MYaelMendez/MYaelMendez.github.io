The estate is the public projection of one sovereign practice: one person,
building capability that runs on his own hardware and is published as open
sourceware.

Every surface exists to make a capability **addressable and verifiable by a
human** — not to impress, and not to assert.

## The one rule

**MEASURED, NOT DECLARED.** A number, count or claim on any surface is either
read from a live source or labelled unverified. A surface that cannot show its
evidence is a draft.

## Where authority lives

Authority stays with the human. Agents propose, measure and publish; they do
not grant, spend or permit.

## The zero point

The origin — `x0:y0:z0:æ0` — is where every address is measured from.
If it lies, everything derived from it lies.

## The rules the charter earned

Each one was written after something broke. They are falsifiable the same way
the clauses above are.

**6 · Declare the interpreter.** A measurement without its environment is a
claim. `12/12 under hermes-venv` is a measurement; `12/12` alone is a slogan.
*Check:* name the interpreter, the stack and the moment, or say nothing.

**7 · A missing dependency is not a broken instrument.** Classify the gap. An
instrument that needs CuPy is green on the GPU interpreter and cannot run on
another — a dependency gap, not a failure. Painting it red hides the difference.
*Check:* is the refusal a `ModuleNotFoundError`? Then it is a gap, and it must
be reported as one.

**8 · Disagreement is a finding, not a pass.** If two interpreters disagree, the
row says `flaky`. Never take the first `ok` and stop. Green is a snapshot at the
moment named — never a property of the thing. *Check:* does the row show every
verdict, or only the convenient one?

**9 · Derive, never hand-write.** If a surface can drift, generate it. A number
typed into markup is a claim; a number read from the registry is a measurement.
*Check:* delete the file and regenerate. If the figure changes, it was typed.

**10 · Counted means listed.** If you say twelve, show twelve. A total that counts
an entry the array omits is a lie about your own shape — and the omission is the
most interesting part. *Check:* `len(entries) == total`. If not, the manifest
is broken.

**11 · The gate must not measure itself.** A gate that re-measures the whole
system inside itself cannot converge, and it times out. Measure the instrument;
gate the publication. *Check:* is the gate faster than what it gates?

## How a clause earns its place

Every clause must be falsifiable. "Measured, not declared" is checkable: is
the figure fetched, or typed? "Sovereign" is checkable: does it run without an
external runtime? A clause that cannot be checked is a slogan, and does not
belong here.

## What this file is for

It is the conductor's contract: the thing that sits between one human and many
agents — translating intent, arbitrating authority, fanning out, fanning in.
A task, a surface or an agent that cannot name the clause it serves is not part
of the estate.
