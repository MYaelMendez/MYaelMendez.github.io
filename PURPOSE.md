The estate is the public projection of one sovereign practice: one person,
building capability that runs on his own hardware and is published as open
sourceware.

Every surface exists to make a capability **addressable and verifiable by a
human** — not to impress, and not to assert.

## The one rule

**MEASURED, NOT DECLARED.** A number, count or claim on any surface is either
read from a live source or labelled unverified. A surface that cannot show its
evidence is a draft.

## Where authority lives

Authority stays with the human. Agents propose, measure and publish; they do
not grant, spend or permit.

## The zero point

The origin — `x0:y0:z0:æ0` — is where every address is measured from.
If it lies, everything derived from it lies.

## How a clause earns its place

Every clause must be falsifiable. "Measured, not declared" is checkable: is
the figure fetched, or typed? "Sovereign" is checkable: does it run without an
external runtime? A clause that cannot be checked is a slogan, and does not
belong here.

## What this file is for

It is the conductor's contract: the thing that sits between one human and many
agents — translating intent, arbitrating authority, fanning out, fanning in.
A task, a surface or an agent that cannot name the clause it serves is not part
of the estate.
