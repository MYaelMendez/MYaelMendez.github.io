Models you can read, run on your own silicon, and receipt.

A live catalog from public APIs, inference inside your browser (WebGPU) or on your own node (Ollama), and a SHA-256 receipt for every run. Zero cloud rent. Evidence ≠ Authority: the page measures, you decide.
