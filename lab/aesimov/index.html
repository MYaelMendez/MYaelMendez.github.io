What we learned

Name it, then build it.Each of Yæl's lines became a feature within the hour. Language came before code.
Cut scope to ship.Three channels became one. The ones we parked are still parked, and nothing broke.
Human stays root, in the UI.Principle became mechanics: a 1.2-second hold, a hashed receipt, and no publish button for agents.
Say only what's true."You write. I check. You approve." Private Client won't be called a drafting agent until it really drafts.
The LAB is the truth.Every change is verified live and byte-identical. A stale base once dropped a folder, so now we read the head before every commit.
Distribution lives in the artifact.The card and receipt travel with every post. Growth needs no ad budget.
Code was never the bottleneck.DNS, a callback URL, SSH, and a snapshot are what stand between the LAB and privateclient.ai.
