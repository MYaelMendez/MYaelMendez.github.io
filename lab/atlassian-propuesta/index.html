1 · Summary

Teachers, catechists and community leaders in rural and peri-urban Latin America are being asked to use AI with weak connectivity, shared phones and little training. Our coalition trains them as multipliers: they learn to use AI agents responsibly, verify what the agent proposes, and teach their own communities, with materials designed for low data (text and voice before video).

Agent proposes → learner checks → human decides.

4 · What we already built (open, in public)

  • Recibo de nivelación: each learner logs what the agent proposed, what they checked and what they decided; it goes to their own email and nothing is stored online. Open
  • Teacher teleprompter: a 60-minute class script any facilitator can run from a phone. Open
  • ZACAPA.DAO: a civic exercise on access versus capacity using real road data for 11 towns in Zacapa, Guatemala. Open
  • Low-data design: one minute of class as text is about 2 KB, as a voice note about 120 KB, as 360p video about 5 MB (approximate).

5 · AI use, risks and safeguards

  • AI is used to propose; a human always checks and decides. No automated grading.
  • No passwords pasted into chats; personal data stays on the learner's device or email.
  • Offline and low-data paths for every lesson; where AI adds no value, we teach without it.
  • Faith-based partners serve everyone regardless of belief; no participation requirement.
