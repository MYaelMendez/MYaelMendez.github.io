one estate · one principal · one rule

1 · what it is

The estate is the public projection of one sovereign practice — one person building capability that runs on his own hardware and is published as open sourceware.

checked how — does it run without an external runtime, and is its source public? If it needs a cloud account to function, it is not in the estate.

2 · who it serves

Every surface exists to make a capability addressable and verifiable by a human. Not to impress, and not to assert.

checked how — can a person reach the capability and see its evidence without an agent explaining it?

3 · the one rule

MEASURED, NOT DECLARED. A number, count or claim on any surface is either read from a live source or labelled unverified. A surface that cannot show its evidence is a draft.

checked how — is the figure fetched, or typed? Typed figures drift; fetched figures are wrong only if their source is wrong.

4 · where authority lives

Authority stays with the human. Agents propose, measure and publish; they do not grant, spend or permit.

checked how — does the act need a human signature, a key, or a yes? If an agent can do it alone, it was never authority.

5 · the zero point

The origin — x0:y0:z0:æ0 — is where every address is measured from. If it lies, everything derived from it lies.

checked how — the origin's own figures are fetched, never frozen (/origin/).
