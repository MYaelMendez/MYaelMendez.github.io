one estate · one principal · one rule

1 · what it is

The estate is the public projection of one sovereign practice — one person building capability that runs on his own hardware and is published as open sourceware.

checked how — does it run without an external runtime, and is its source public? If it needs a cloud account to function, it is not in the estate.

2 · who it serves

Every surface exists to make a capability addressable and verifiable by a human. Not to impress, and not to assert.

checked how — can a person reach the capability and see its evidence without an agent explaining it?

3 · the one rule

MEASURED, NOT DECLARED. A number, count or claim on any surface is either read from a live source or labelled unverified. A surface that cannot show its evidence is a draft.

checked how — is the figure fetched, or typed? Typed figures drift; fetched figures are wrong only if their source is wrong.

4 · where authority lives

Authority stays with the human. Agents propose, measure and publish; they do not grant, spend or permit. The order is fixed and named:

HUMAN://ROOT → æ POLICY → wætz:// → robot:// → {asimov|reachy|microduck|meshbot}:// → RECEIPT

wætz:// is the proprietary policy; robot:// is the open dialect it governs. An address is not a permission.

checked how — does the act need a human signature, a key, or a yes? If an agent can do it alone, it was never authority. A scheme that outranks another must say so.

5 · the zero point

The origin — x0:y0:z0:æ0 — is where every address is measured from. If it lies, everything derived from it lies.

checked how — the origin's own figures are fetched, never frozen (/origin/).
the rules the charter earned · each one paid for by a defect

6 · declare the interpreter

A measurement without its environment is a claim. Name the interpreter, the stack and the moment — 12/12 under hermes-venv is a measurement; 12/12 alone is a slogan.

earned when — the same chain read 12/12 from a shell and 11/12 when a different interpreter resolved as python. The number had not changed; the environment had.

7 · a missing dependency is not a broken instrument

Classify the gap. An instrument that needs CuPy is green on the GPU interpreter and cannot run on another — that is a dependency gap, not a failure, and painting it red hides the difference.

earned when — five instruments were reported flaky because the refusal sat at checks.cuda.error, not at the top level. Reading only the top level turned a missing module into a disagreement.

8 · disagreement is a finding, not a pass

If two interpreters disagree, the row says flaky. Never take the first ok and stop. Green is a snapshot at the moment named — never a property of the thing.

earned when — one instrument refused in 0.45s with no reason at all. A silent refusal is a finding too, and it must be shown rather than averaged away.

9 · derive, never hand-write

If a surface can drift, generate it. A number typed into markup is a claim; a number read from the registry is a measurement. Delete the file and regenerate — if the figure changes, it was typed.

earned when — a headquarters page said 20 ok · 0 refused while the registry measured 21/18/3. Three surfaces, three answers, one measured source.

10 · counted means listed

If you say twelve, show twelve. A total that counts an entry the array omits is a lie about your own shape — and the omission is the most interesting part.

earned when — links_total: 12 with len(links): 11. The twelfth was counted, sealed into the total, and never listed.

11 · the gate must not measure itself

A gate that re-measures the whole system inside itself cannot converge, and it times out. Measure the instrument; gate the publication. If the gate takes longer than what it gates, it is the wrong gate.

earned when — the family's own doctor timed out at 120s while the family took 5m35s, and its verdict was recorded in the surfaces it was supposed to judge. It now runs in 0.4s.
