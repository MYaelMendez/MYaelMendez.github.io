# OSW.os — Account Requirements & Independence Notice

> Version 1.0 · Proposed onboarding policy · OpenSourceWare (OSW)

## Before you start

**Two accounts. One constructor.**

For the full GitHub-native OSW.os constructor workflow, bring:

1. **Your own ChatGPT/OpenAI account** to use the conversational cockpit and the AI capabilities available on your plan.
2. **Your own GitHub account** to fork, inspect, version, and publish your constructor repository, subject to repository and plan permissions.

**Paid subscriptions are not universally required.** Free plans may be sufficient for introductory workflows. Some advanced AI capabilities, usage limits, private infrastructure, integrations, or workflows may require paid plans or separate services. OSW.os must show those requirements before asking the user to proceed.

## Independence and affiliation

OSW.os / OpenSourceWare is an **independent initiative**, not an official OpenAI or GitHub product. Use of these services does not imply their sponsorship, endorsement, partnership, or approval. Users must comply with applicable provider terms and brand policies.

## Human ownership and authority

- **🫵 is the human principal.** Agents may perform bounded, delegated work but do not become legal principals.
- Users control their own repositories, credentials, permission grants, and authorized actions, subject to service terms, contracts, and applicable intellectual-property rights.
- A repository fork is not by itself proof of exclusive intellectual-property ownership or legal entity formation.
- Connecting an account does **not** authorize spending, publishing, repository changes, filings, external communication, or vehicle control. Each consequential action requires the appropriate authorization.
- **Context may propagate; authority does not.**

## Data, receipts, and QR continuity

- A 🧾 witness receipt must distinguish verified work from proposals, simulations, and unverified claims.
- An æQR³ code may reference a checkpoint but must not contain secrets or grant permissions.
- Durable checkpoint storage and cross-device restoration require implemented persistence, access control, and authentication. Do not promise resume functionality until verified.
