## Purpose

A public, open-source infrastructure that helps one human create, own, and operate an entire agentic environment.

The next breakthrough isn't another concept, domain, or prototype. It's getting our existing components to work together so reliably that someone other than us can use them.

## The five horizons ahead

### 01 · Unify the origin

Make `MYaelMendez.github.io` the canonical engineering source, preserving the existing pages, æQR, CLI, and Studio. Use reviewable branches to consolidate overlapping work without destroying historical implementations.

**Outcome:** One navigable architecture, with a traceable version history.

### 02 · Implement Fæscia

Move `>_æææ://fæscia` from architectural metaphor into a real, tested connective layer with typed environment manifests, capability discovery, provenance, and explicit permission boundaries.

**Outcome:** Different agents and widgets can exchange bounded context without inheriting authority.

### 03 · Build the first complete agentic business

Combine PrivateClient.ai, LLM.store, CLI.LLC, and æ Studio into a demonstrable one-person company workflow: discover a capability, configure an environment, review proposed work, and generate a verifiable artifact.

**Outcome:** One real use case, rather than ten disconnected demonstrations.

These are integration goals, not assertions that identity services, business formation, payments, or runtime connections are already operational.

### 04 · Turn the platform into a school

Make #eLearnLATAM and #emprendeLATAM pathways where people start with emojis, graduate to agentic workflows, and publish to their own GitHub or domain.

**Outcome:** Students become builders, and builders become teachers.

Publishing remains a separate, human-reviewed step. Keep private learner, family, and founder information out of public artifacts.

### 05 · Open the ecosystem

Let others fork, extend, and publish agentic environments through æ.store and discover capabilities through LLM.store, using the same Fæscia contracts.

**Outcome:** A network of human-owned environments rather than one centrally operated application.

Forkability, redistribution, and provider integration must respect the licenses and permissions applicable to each component. This roadmap does not grant rights over third-party services or assets.
