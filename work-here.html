F1 Metaphordriver retains responsibility

In Formula 1, the driver doesn't build the engine, calculate every sensor reading, or personally operate the pit wall. A coordinated system supports the driver. Your agentic environment follows the same pattern.

Human = Driver · Agents = Team · Tools = Machinery · Telemetry = Intelligence · Witness = Race Record
The driver retains responsibility for decisions, and an agent's available capability is not automatic permission to execute. Context may propagate. Authority does not.
