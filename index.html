mobility.foundation — the walk as one 4D object

Everything else here displays or describes. This is the only part that touches physics. A trajectory is a four-dimensional object — x, y, z, t — so every pose of the walk is drawn at once, along the path it actually took. The pose nearest the moving time-slice is solid; the rest dim by temporal distance.

æææ:// — the policy drives it, not an animator. Menlo's locomotion ONNX against MuJoCo contact: 78 observations in, 23 joint targets out, four substeps per 50 Hz tick. The base trajectory emerges from control; nothing prescribed it.

The verified run, from proof.json inside the package:

+4.1868 m base travel +4.1743 m COM travel 600 policy ticks · 2400 substeps min base height 0.6125 m no falls

What the proof does not yet measure: every field above is a survival metric. Foot clearance, step length, symmetry and cost of transport are absent — so a robot that shuffles 4.19 m without falling scores success: true. Ours clears the ground by about 1.5 cm. Naming that is the next iteration, not a footnote.
