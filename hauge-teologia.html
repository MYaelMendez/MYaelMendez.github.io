LINEAGE://1771—1824

A Life in Motion

1771
Born in Norway

Hauge was born into a farming family near Fredrikstad, in a deeply Lutheran society.

1796
Spiritual awakening

A profound religious experience redirected his life toward preaching, writing and service.

1796+
Walking preacher

He traveled extensively through Norway, preaching and forming networks of lay believers.

1800s
Faith meets enterprise

Haugeans became associated with farms, mills, printing, shipping, workshops, commerce and local development.

1824
Legacy continues

Hauge died, but the movement continued shaping Norwegian religious, civic and economic life.
